# Data source register

Research date: 16 November 2026. This register covers **data** the app shows. Services the app calls (realtime
feeds, ticketing, payments, maps, notifications, AI) are in `API_INTEGRATION_REGISTER.md`.

Rule: data reaches users only through a recorded source with a known licence. Each import keeps its source, licence
note and import date, and every screen that shows data shows its classification and last-updated date.

## Freshness and expiry
- GTFS feeds: the admin dashboard warns 30 days before `feed_end_date`; expired feeds are labelled "expired" and are not
  used for "now" planning.
- Realtime: positions older than 2 minutes are "stale", older than 10 minutes are hidden.
- Information pages (passes, NCMC, emergency, airport): each shows the date it was last checked against its source.
