# Privacy policy — Bengaluru Transit Planner

> **Draft for professional review.** This is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It describes what the software actually does as of 16 November 2026.

**Who we are.** Bengaluru Transit Planner is an independent, unofficial app developed by Mopuru Upendra Reddy,
Bengaluru. It is not run by BMTC, BMRCL or any government body. Contact: [developer email to be added before publication].

## What we collect, and why
| Data | Where it is kept | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| A random device key (no name, phone or email) | Your browser; the server keeps only a one-way hash | To keep your saved routes and scenarios separate from other people's | Until you use "Delete my data" or clear the browser |
| Saved routes, future scenarios, preferences you create | Server, linked to the device-key hash | To show them to you again | Until you delete them or use "Delete my data" |
| Favourite stops, recent searches, language, theme and other settings | Your browser only | Convenience | Until you clear them, use "Delete my data", or clear the browser |
| Searches and journey requests | Not stored by the app | To answer the request | Not kept by the app (see hosting below) |
| Your location, only when you tap "Stops near me" | Sent with that one request; not stored by the app | To find stops near you | Not kept by the app |
| Security and audit records of administrative actions | Server | Accountability for data changes | See the data retention policy |

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, date of birth or payment details. We do not use analytics,
advertising or tracking scripts, and we do not sell or share data.

## Third parties that receive technical data
- **Hosting provider** (currently Render): receives every request, including your IP address, as part of serving the app.
- **OpenStreetMap tile servers**: receive your IP address and the map area when a map is shown.
- **Official websites you open from the app** (BMTC, BMRCL, the airport and others) receive your visit under their own policies.
See `THIRD_PARTY_SERVICES_REGISTER.md`.

## Your choices and rights
- Use the app without sharing your location: search by stop name instead.
- **Delete my data** (My account) erases everything the server holds for your device and clears this device's lists.
- Ask a question or make a request at the contact address above.

## Legal context (for the reviewer)
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified November 2025) apply to
digital personal data. Reported phasing: most duties of data fiduciaries (notice, security safeguards, breach
notification, erasure, rights of data principals) apply from about 13 May 2027 — confirm the dates against the
Gazette notification. This app is designed to meet them early: minimal data, no identity collected, erasure on request.
