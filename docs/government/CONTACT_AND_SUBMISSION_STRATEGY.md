# Contact and submission strategy

**No email has been sent and no application submitted.** Each step below needs the owner's approval.

## Outcomes are different — never treat them as the same
| Outcome | What it means | Who could grant it |
|---|---|---|
| Technical feedback | Officials comment on the app | Any office below |
| Data-sharing permission | A written licence to use their data | BMTC, BMRCL, DULT (data owners) |
| Pilot approval | Written permission for a limited trial | BMTC/BMRCL/DULT, as relevant |
| Vendor registration | Being eligible to bid for government work | E-procurement portal of the department |
| Government procurement | A paid contract after a tender | The department, by tender |
| Formal endorsement | Public statement of support | Senior officials, in writing |
| Official partnership | A signed agreement | The organisation, in writing |

## Order and timeline (target: before 1 January 2027)
1. **Week of 17 Nov:** owner creates the 4.0 staging service; release-signs the APK; tests on a real phone.
2. **Week of 24 Nov:** fill the bracketed fields in `letters/`; generate the PDFs; owner reviews and signs.
3. **By 5 Dec:** send DULT (data hub + licence) and BMTC (GTFS) letters by post and email with the PDFs attached;
   keep the dispatch receipts.
4. **By 12 Dec:** BMRCL letter.
5. **Follow-up:** a polite phone call 10 working days after delivery; offer a 20-minute demonstration.
6. **Data received:** import as OFFICIAL only after verifying it against the publisher's source, record the licence,
   update the audit, and only then propose the pilot.

## What not to do
Do not use logos of BMTC, BMRCL or the Government; do not describe the project as approved, partnered or endorsed;
do not contact officials' personal numbers; do not use reverse-engineered APIs.
